About the Project

AI Career Guidance Assistant

A student project aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goal 8 — Decent Work and Economic Growth.

Our Mission

Bridging the Career Guidance Gap

Millions of students and fresh graduates worldwide struggle to navigate career choices. Without personalised guidance, many make decisions based on limited information, leading to skills mismatches and prolonged unemployment.

This platform provides free, AI-assisted career exploration, skill development resources, and job preparation tools — helping anyone with internet access take their first confident steps toward meaningful employment.

Project Plan

Lean Canvas

This canvas describes the project's current capabilities and possible future directions. Revenue and partnerships shown below are illustrative future possibilities only — they do not currently exist.

1. Problem
  • Career confusion and lack of personalised guidance
  • Unclear link between studies and job-market skills
  • Limited access to free, structured career resources
4. Solution
  • AI-assisted career assessment and recommendations
  • Skill gap analysis with prioritised learning plans
  • Structured roadmaps and project ideas
  • Resume and interview preparation tools
3. Unique Value Proposition

One free platform for personalised career exploration, skill development, and job preparation — designed specifically for students and fresh graduates.

2. Customer Segments
  • University and college students
  • Recent graduates seeking first jobs
  • Internship seekers
  • Career changers and self-learners
5. Channels
  • Public website (this platform)
  • College and university placement cells
  • Student communities and social media
  • Open-source promotion on GitHub
